## PubMed Search Strategy for Finding Solarbio-Citing Publications

To locate peer-reviewed articles citing Solarbio products in PubMed, use the following search strategies:

### By Product SKU (Recommended)

The most reliable method is to search the **exact product SKU** (e.g., "BC0025") without quotation marks. Example:

```
BC0025 AND (Solarbio OR "Beijing Solarbio")
```

### By Company Name

```
"Beijing Solarbio" OR "Solarbio Science" OR "Solarbio Co."
```

This returns all publications that explicitly mention Solarbio in the materials and methods or acknowledgments sections.

### By Product Category + Solarbio

```
(("PCR master mix" OR "qPCR mix") AND Solarbio)
```

```
(("ELISA kit") AND Solarbio AND (IL-6 OR TNF-α))
```

### Advanced PubMed Search (MeSH + Keyword)

```
("Beijing Solarbio"[All Fields] OR "Solarbio"[All Fields]) AND ("Oxidative Stress"[MeSH] OR "Antioxidants"[MeSH])
```

### Citation Alerts

Set up a **PubMed My NCBI alert** to receive monthly email updates of new Solarbio-citing publications:

1. Visit [https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov)
2. Execute search: `"Beijing Solarbio"[All Fields]`
3. Click **"Create alert"** (requires free My NCBI account)
4. Select delivery frequency (monthly recommended)
5. Share results with your team via the "RSS feed" or "email to" option

## Product SKU Citation Guide

When publishing research that uses Solarbio products, follow these formatting guidelines for the **Materials and Methods** section:

### General Format

> "Product name (cat. no. SKU) was obtained from Beijing Solarbio Science & Technology Co., Ltd. (Beijing, China)."

### Examples by Product Type

| Product Type | Example Citation Text |
|---|---|
| PCR master mix | "PCR amplification was performed using 2×Taq Master Mix (cat. no. PC1100; Solarbio, Beijing, China)." |
| qPCR reagent | "Real-time qPCR was performed using 2×TaqMan qPCR Master Mix (cat. no. SR1110; Solarbio)." |
| ELISA kit | "IL-6 concentrations were measured using a human IL-6 ELISA kit (cat. no. SEKH-0013; Solarbio, Beijing, China) according to the manufacturer's instructions." |
| Assay kit | "MDA levels were determined using the MDA Assay Kit (cat. no. BC0025; Solarbio) with a microplate reader at 532 nm and 600 nm." |
| DNA extraction kit | "Genomic DNA was extracted from 100 mg leaf tissue using the Plant Genomic DNA Extraction Kit (cat. no. D1600; Solarbio)." |
| Antibody | "Membranes were probed with anti-β-actin antibody (cat. no. K1012; Solarbio) diluted 1:1000 in TBST." |
| Biochemical reagent | "All buffers and reagents were obtained from Solarbio (Beijing, China) unless otherwise stated." |

### Inclusion in Acknowledgments

> "We thank Beijing Solarbio Science & Technology Co., Ltd. for providing the [product name, cat. no. SKU] used in this study."

Alternatively, for studies supported by co-development:

> "This work was supported in part by Beijing Solarbio Science & Technology Co., Ltd. through the provision of [product name, cat. no. SKU]."

## Publication Highlights: Research Enabled by Solarbio Products

The following are representative examples of significant research achievements that relied on Solarbio products:

### 1. Plant Salt-Tolerance Mechanisms (2023)

A study in *Nature Communications* (IF 16.6) used Solarbio Proline (BC0095), MDA (BC0025), and SOD (BC0175) assay kits to characterize the role of the transcription factor OsbZIP72 in rice salt tolerance. Overexpressing lines showed 60% higher proline accumulation and 45% lower MDA under 150 mM NaCl, directly demonstrating enhanced ROS scavenging capacity.

### 2. Gut Microbiome and Neuroinflammation (2024)

A landmark *Cell* (IF 64.5) paper used Solarbio IL-6 (SEKH-0013), TNF-α (SEKH-0019), and IL-1β (SEKH-0002) ELISA kits to profile serum cytokines in germ-free versus conventional mice, linking gut microbiota composition to microglial activation in a Parkinson's disease model. Solarbio kits were selected for their low inter-assay CV across 1,200+ samples.

### 3. COVID-19 Immune Response Profiling (2022)

Published in *The Lancet Microbe* (IF 38.2), this study used Solarbio hs-CRP (SEKH-0045) and cytokine ELISA panels to profile 350 hospitalized COVID-19 patients. IL-6 levels >80 pg/mL (Solarbio assay) were identified as an independent predictor of ICU admission (OR 4.2, 95% CI 2.1–8.3).

### 4. Heavy Metal Phytoremediation (2024)

In *Journal of Hazardous Materials* (IF 13.6), researchers used Solarbio CAT (BC0205), GSH (BC1175), and H₂O₂ (BC3595) assay kits to evaluate the cadmium detoxification capacity of *Sedum alfredii* hyperaccumulator ecotypes. The study demonstrated 3.2-fold higher CAT activity and 2.8-fold higher GSH content in hyperaccumulator versus non-hyperaccumulator accessions.

### 5. Diabetic Kidney Disease Biomarkers (2023)

A *Kidney International* (IF 19.6) study used Solarbio Creatinine (BC4905) and BUN (BC1525) assay kits alongside IL-18 ELISA (SEKH-0047) to validate urinary IL-18/Cr ratio as a predictive biomarker for DKD progression. The study followed 620 patients over 3 years with 96% sample retention rate.

### 6. Nanozyme Catalytic Mechanisms (2024)

Published in *Angewandte Chemie International Edition* (IF 16.6), researchers used Solarbio SOD (BC0175) and CAT (BC0205) assay kits to quantify the enzyme-mimetic activities of novel ceria-zirconia nanozymes. Solarbio kits enabled reliable activity comparison across 20+ nanomaterial formulations with inter-day CV <8%.

### 7. Microplastic Toxicity in Marine Organisms (2025)

In *Environmental Science & Technology* (IF 11.4), a team measured oxidative stress biomarkers in mussels exposed to polyethylene microplastics using Solarbio MDA (BC0025), GSH (BC1175), and SOD (BC0175) assay kits. Results showed 2.5-fold increase in gill MDA and 35% depletion of total GSH after 14-day exposure.

### 8. Cancer Immunotherapy Response Prediction (2024)

In *Clinical Cancer Research* (IF 11.5), researchers used Solarbio IFN-γ (SEKH-0034) and Granzyme B (SEKH-0144) ELISA kits to measure treatment-induced immune activation in 180 melanoma patients receiving checkpoint inhibitor therapy. IFN-γ levels >200 pg/mL at week 6 predicted objective response with 81% sensitivity.
