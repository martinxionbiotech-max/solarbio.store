## Quality and Regulatory Compliance

All molecular biology reagents are manufactured under controlled conditions with:

- Raw material qualification: Each enzyme lot tested for exonuclease, endonuclease, and nickase activity before use
- In-process controls: Reaction assembly, thermocycling, and detection validated per batch
- Final release testing: Activity assays using standardized reference templates (human GAPDH, bacterial 16S rDNA)
- Sterility testing: All solutions filtered through 0.22 µm membranes and tested for microbial contamination
- DNase/RNase testing: Fluorogenic substrate assays with detection limit <1 pg DNase/RNase per reaction
- Traceability: Each lot tracked by raw material batch, manufacturing date, and QC analyst
