## Quality Control Testing

Each lot of immunology reagent undergoes comprehensive QC:

- **ELISA kits**: Calibrator recovery (90–110%), precision (CV <10% intra, <15% inter), linearity of dilution (80–120% recovery), spike recovery in relevant matrices, and cross-reactivity panel testing
- **Antibodies**: SDS-PAGE under reducing/non-reducing conditions, Western blot validation using positive control lysates, IHC optimization on formalin-fixed paraffin-embedded tissue controls, and lot-to-lot consistency verification
- **IHC detection systems**: Sensitivity verification using known positive tissue controls, background assessment on negative tissue controls, and stability testing at 37°C for 7 days (accelerated aging)
