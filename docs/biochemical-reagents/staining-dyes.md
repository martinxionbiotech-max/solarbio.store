# Technical Specification: Staining Dyes & Histology Reagents

## 1. Product Overview

Solarbio staining dyes and histology reagents cover routine H&E staining, special stains for histochemical analysis, and fluorescent dyes for microscopy and flow cytometry. All products are validated on control tissue sections with lot-specific staining patterns documented.

## 3. Recommended Protocols

### 3.1 H&E Staining (Paraffin Sections)

| Step | Reagent | Time | Technical Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deparaffinize | Xylene | 10 min × 2 | Fresh xylene for second wash |
| Rehydrate | 100%→95%→80%→70% ethanol | 5 min each | Gradual to avoid tissue damage |
| Nuclear stain | Harris hematoxylin | 5–8 min | Filter before use to remove oxidized surface |
| Blue in tap water | Running tap water | 5–10 min | Water should be alkaline (pH 8–9) for bluing |
| Differentiate | 1% HCl-EtOH | 2–3 s | Quick dip, then water rinse |
| Counterstain | Eosin Y | 1–2 min | Alcoholic eosin preferred for sharper contrast |
| Dehydrate | 95%→100%→xylene | 3 min each | Complete dehydration critical for permanent mounts |
| Mount | Resinous mounting medium | — | Use toluene-based; avoid bubble formation |

### 3.2 DAPI Staining

| Step | Detail | Technical Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Fix cells | 4% paraformaldehyde, 15 min RT | Freshly prepared PFA for best nuclear morphology |
| Wash | PBS, 3 × 5 min | Gentle agitation |
| Permeabilize | 0.1% Triton X-100, 10 min | Adjust for thicker sections: 0.3% Triton, 30 min |
| Stain | DAPI (0.5–1 µg/mL in PBS), 5 min RT, dark | Higher concentration for tissue sections (2 µg/mL) |
| Wash | PBS, 3 × 5 min | Thorough wash reduces background |
| Mount | Anti-fade mounting medium | Use glycerol-based for long-term storage at -20°C |

### 3.3 Oil Red O Staining (Frozen Sections)

| Step | Detail | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Section preparation | Cryosection at 5–10 µm, air-dry | 30 min |
| Rinse | 60% isopropanol | 5 min |
| Stain | Oil Red O working solution (0.5% in isopropanol, diluted 3:2 in water) | 10–15 min |
| Rinse | 60% isopropanol | 5 min (until background clear) |
| Nuclear counterstain | Hematoxylin (Mayer's), optional | 1–2 min |
| Mount | Aqueous mounting medium (glycerol-gelatin) | — |

## 6. Troubleshooting Guide

| Issue | Likely Cause | Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Hematoxylin fading | Oxidized hematoxylin | Replace; filter before use |
| Eosin too intense | Overstained or insufficient dehydration | Reduce eosin time; ensure complete ethanol steps |
| DAPI high background | Insufficient washing after staining | Increase PBS washes to 5 × 5 min |
| Oil Red O non-specific precipitate | Dye crystals formed | Filter working solution through 0.45 µm |
| PAS magenta too weak | Periodic acid oxidation insufficient | Extend oxidation to 10–15 min at 37°C |
| Masson's trichrome inconsistent | Bouin's fixation inadequate | Ensure Bouin's at 56°C for 60 min or overnight at RT |
