## Custom Buffer Formulation — Ordering Workflow

Solarbio accepts custom buffer formulation requests for laboratories with specific ionic composition, pH, osmolality, or additive requirements.

### Workflow Steps

1. **Submit specification request** via [solarbio.store](https://solarbio.store) or email sales@solarbio.store using the template: (a) buffer name, (b) final volume required, (c) component list with mM or g/L, (d) target pH ± tolerance, (e) target osmolality (if applicable), (f) preferred packaging, (g) sterility requirement (0.1 µm or 0.22 µm filtered), (h) endotoxin limit if <0.5 EU/mL needed.
2. **Technical review** (2 business days): Our formulation team reviews the components for compatibility issues (e.g., Ca²⁺/Mg²⁺ precipitation risk with phosphate, microbial growth potential with glucose-containing buffers).
3. **Quotation**: A custom SKU is created with per-lot pricing. Minimum order: 1 L for simple buffers, 5 L for complex formulations.
4. **Manufacturing** (5–10 business days): ISO 9001:2015 controlled production with in-process pH and conductivity checks.
5. **Release testing**: pH ± 0.1, osmolality ± 5%, conductivity ± 5%, sterility (if specified), endotoxin (if specified), DNase/RNase (if specified).
6. **COA delivery**: Electronic COA with all measured values, lot number, and expiry date.

**Supported custom additives**: EDTA, EGTA, DTT, TCEP, PMSF, protease inhibitor cocktail, sodium azide (0.02%), glycerol (up to 50% v/v), sucrose, Triton X-100, Tween 20, SDS, BSA (up to 5%). Any additive compatible with 0.1 µm or 0.22 µm filtration can be included.

## Recommended Storage and Handling

- **Buffers**: Store at room temperature (15–30°C) unless otherwise labeled. After opening, store 2–8°C for buffers containing biological components (e.g., PBS with Ca²⁺/Mg²⁺).
- **Small molecules**: Keep sealed under inert gas; desiccate at −20°C. Light-sensitive compounds should be stored in amber vials.
- **Analytical standards**: Store in original container, tightly sealed. Desiccate where moisture sensitivity is indicated.
- **Staining dyes**: Protect from light. Hematoxylin and eosin at RT; fluorescent dyes at −20°C.
