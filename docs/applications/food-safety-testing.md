## Key Assays and Specifications

### Heavy Metal Detection

| Metal | Assay Principle | Detection Limit | Linear Range | Sample Preparation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lead (Pb²⁺) | Colorimetric: dithizone-based | 0.1 mg/L | 0.1–5 mg/L | Acid digestion (HNO₃/HClO₄) |
| Cadmium (Cd²⁺) | Colorimetric: dithizone-based | 0.05 mg/L | 0.05–2 mg/L | Acid digestion |
| Copper (Cu²⁺) | Colorimetric: BCA-based | 0.1 mg/L | 0.1–10 mg/L | Wet ashing |
| Iron (Fe³⁺) | Colorimetric: ferrozine-based | 0.05 mg/L | 0.05–5 mg/L | Acid extraction |

### Pesticide Residue ELISA

- Sensitivity: LOD 0.01–0.1 ng/mL depending on target pesticide
- Cross-reactivity: Tested against panel of 20–30 structurally related compounds
- Matrix validation: Grains, fruits, vegetables, tea leaves, soil, water
- Extraction: QuEChERS-based (acetonitrile extraction + dispersive SPE cleanup)
- Assay time: 1.5–2 hours (competitive format)
- Recovery: 80–120% for spiked samples at regulatory limits

## Sample Preparation Protocols

### Grain/Nut Extraction (for mycotoxins)

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Grinding | Mill to pass through 1 mm sieve |
| Extraction solvent | 70% methanol (v/v) for aflatoxins; water for DON |
| Ratio | 10 mL solvent per 1 g ground sample |
| Agitation | Shake vigorously for 30 min at RT |
| Filtration | Whatman #1 or equivalent filter paper |
| Dilution | Dilute filtrate in sample buffer (1:5 to 1:20 as needed) |

### Fruit/Vegetable Extraction (for pesticides)

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Weigh | 10 g homogenized sample |
| Add | 10 mL acetonitrile + 1 g NaCl + 4 g MgSO₄ |
| Vortex | 1 min, then centrifuge 5 min at 4000 × g |
| Cleanup | Transfer 1 mL supernatant to d-SPE tube (PSA + C₁₈) |
| Buffering | Transfer cleaned supernatant, dilute 1:10 in ELISA assay buffer |
