# Product Storage & Shelf Life

## Storage by Category

| Product Category | Storage Temperature | Special Handling | Shelf Life (Unopened) | Shelf Life After Opening |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PCR Master Mixes | -20°C | Avoid multiple freeze-thaw | 24 months | 12 months (aliquot recommended) |
| qPCR Master Mixes | -20°C | Light-sensitive, dark container | 24 months | 6 months (aliquot; ≤5 freeze-thaw cycles) |
| DNA Polymerases | -20°C | Aliquot to avoid freeze-thaw | 24 months | 12 months |
| DNA Extraction Kits | RT (15–30°C) | Keep dry, sealed | 24 months | 12 months |
| RNA Extraction Kits | RT (15–30°C) | Avoid RNase contamination | 24 months | 12 months |
| Competent Cells | -80°C (long-term); -20°C (<1 month) | Do not thaw until use | 12 months | Single-use only |
| ELISA Kits | 2–8°C | Do not freeze | 12 months | Use entire kit within 1 month of opening |
| Antibodies | -20°C or 2–8°C | Aliquot if repeated use | 24 months | 12 months (glycerol-containing); 3 months (PBS-only) |
| Assay Kits | 2–8°C or as labeled | Check each component | 6–12 months | Kit-specific (usually 1 month) |
| Buffers & Solutions | RT (15–30°C) | After opening, store 2–8°C | 24 months unopened | 6 months |
| Small Molecules | -20°C | Desiccate, N₂ blanket | 24 months | 12 months (stock solution in DMSO: 1–3 months) |
| Analytical Standards | 2–8°C or as labeled | Desiccate, tightly sealed | 36 months | 6 months after opening (verify before use) |
| Staining Dyes | RT or -20°C | Light-sensitive, dark container | 12–24 months | 6–12 months |
| Bovine Sera | -20°C | Avoid repeated freeze-thaw | 24 months | 6 months (thawed, stored 2–8°C) |

## Freeze-Thaw Stability

| Product Type | Recommended Freeze-Thaw Cycles | Storage Format Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| Taq DNA polymerase (in mix) | ≤ 20 cycles | 50 µL single-use aliquots |
| qPCR master mixes | ≤ 5 cycles | 100 µL single-use aliquots |
| Reverse transcriptase | ≤ 5 cycles | 10 µL single-use aliquots |
| Proteinase K | ≤ 10 cycles | 100 µL aliquots |
| Antibodies | ≤ 3 cycles (aliquot recommended) | 5–10 µL per aliquot |
| Bovine sera | ≤ 3 cycles | 50 mL aliquots |
| Cell lysates | ≤ 3 cycles | Single-use aliquots |
| Primers (100 µM stock) | ≤ 10 cycles | 10 µL working aliquots + 100 µL master stock |
| Small molecule stock solutions | ≤ 5 cycles | 10–20 µL aliquots |

## Temperature Stability Data (Accelerated)

| Product Category | 4°C (short-term) | 25°C | 37°C | 56°C |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2×Taq PCR Mix | 1 month (stable) | 7 days (>95% activity) | 48 h (>90% activity) | 2 h (50% activity loss) |
| SYBR Green qPCR Mix | 2 weeks (>90% activity) | 48 h (>95%) | 24 h (>90%) | 1 h (significant loss) |
| DNA (purified) | 6 months | 1 month | 2 weeks | 1 week |
| BSA (standard) | 12 months | 6 months | 1 month | 1 week |
| Small molecule (DMSO stock) | 3 months | 1 month | 1 week | 48 h |

## Light Sensitivity

Products that require light-protected storage (amber vials, foil-wrapped, dark container):

| Product Type | Light Sensitivity Level | Protection Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| DAPI, Hoechst dyes | High | Foil-wrapped, -20°C |
| FITC and FITC conjugates | High | Amber tubes, -20°C |
| SYBR Green in qPCR mixes | Moderate | Dark container at all times |
| Fluorescent probes (DCFH-DA, JC-1, Fluo-4) | High | Desiccated, dark, -20°C |
| Oil Red O | Moderate | Amber bottle, RT |
| Curcumin, resveratrol | Moderate | Amber vial, -20°C |
| qPCR master mixes (SYBR) | Moderate | Manufacturer's dark tube |

## Expiration Management

- Unopened products should not be used beyond the printed expiration date
- After opening, record the opening date on the vial/label
- Discard if any of the following is observed:
  - Visible precipitation, turbidity, or discoloration (solutions)
  - Clumping or moisture absorption (powders)
  - Change in pH by >0.3 units (buffers)
  - Unexpected assay results (kits, enzymes, antibodies)
  - Growth of microbial contamination (media, buffers)
- For products without printed expiration, apply the general rule: unopened = 24 months from receipt; opened = 6 months
