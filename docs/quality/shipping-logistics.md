## Cold Chain Validation

Solarbio cold chain shipping has been validated for:

- **2–8°C shipments**: Maintain refrigerated temperature for 48 h ambient exposure using 0.9 kg phase-change gel packs per shipper
- **−20°C shipments**: Maintain frozen temperature for 36 h ambient exposure using 1.5 kg frozen gel packs per shipper
- **−78.5°C (dry ice)**: Maintain below −50°C for 72 h using 5 kg dry ice and EPS cryo-shipper
- **−196°C (LN₂ vapor)**: Maintain below −150°C for 10–14 days in validated LN₂ dry shipper

All validation: Temperature data loggers placed in three positions (top, middle, bottom) per test shipment, ≥3 replicate tests per shipper type.

## Temperature Excursion Handling SOP

If a temperature excursion is suspected or confirmed upon receipt, follow these steps:

### Step 1: Immediate Assessment

| Indicator Observed | Action |
|---|---|
| Irreversible temperature indicator shows "EXCURSION" (color change) | Photograph indicator; note time of discovery; do not open package |
| Temperature data logger reading outside acceptable range | Download logger data; identify duration and magnitude of excursion |
| Product appears thawed (frozen products) | Photograph product condition; do not refreeze |
| Cold pack liquid or ice present outside primary container | Photograph; document possible thermal compromise |
| Dry ice completely sublimated | Weigh residual dry ice (if any); calculate sublimation rate |

### Step 2: Contact Solarbio Customer Service

Within 2 h of discovery, contact <service@solarbio.com> with:

- Order number and SKU(s) affected
- Lot number(s) from product label
- Photographs of: outer packaging, temperature indicator(s), product vials/kits, shipping label
- Temperature logger data file (if available)
- Date and time of receipt

### Step 3: Temporary Storage

| Excursion Type | Temporary Storage Instructions |
|---|---|
| 2–8°C product to ambient (≤4 h) | Return to 2–8°C immediately; mark for performance verification |
| 2–8°C product to ambient (>4 h) | Store at 2–8°C; do not use until Solarbio QC reviews data |
| −20°C to ambient (thawed, ice crystals present) | Return to −20°C; mark for QC evaluation |
| −20°C to ambient (fully thawed) | Do not refreeze; store at 4°C if possible; await QC instructions |
| Dry ice shipment (dry ice depleted, warm) | Do not use; QC replacement will be arranged |

### Step 4: Product Disposition

| Assessment Outcome | Action |
|---|---|
| Acceptable (excursion < tolerance, verified) | Product approved for use; note in lab records |
| Conditional (marginal excursion) | Performance testing recommended before use (comparison with retained reference) |
| Unacceptable (excursion exceeds tolerance) | Replacement product shipped at no charge (within 5 business days); affected product destroyed per laboratory waste protocols |

### Step 5: Documentation

All excursions are logged in the Solarbio CAPA system. Root cause analysis is performed quarterly to identify shipping lane and seasonal trends.

## LN₂ Shipping for Cryopreserved Products

Solarbio LN₂ dry shippers are used for transport of cryopreserved competent cells, cell lines, and other −196°C-sensitive products.

### LN₂ Dry Shipper Specifications

| Parameter | Specification |
|---|---|
| Shipper type | MVE SC 2.0/2.5 or equivalent vapor-phase LN₂ shipper |
| LN₂ absorption capacity | 1.5–2.5 kg in absorbent matrix |
| Hold time (static, no LN₂ refill) | 14–21 days (depending on model) |
| Temperature at sample position | −196°C (liquid); <−150°C (vapor phase) |
| Sample capacity | 12–25 cryovials (1.0–2.0 mL) per shipper |
| Outer packaging | UN-certified overpack box (UN 4G) |

### LN₂ Shipping Procedure

1. **Charging**: Fill shipper with LN₂ 24 h before use; allow to equilibrate; top off immediately before packing.
2. **Sample loading**: Place cryovials in inventory cassettes; immerse in LN₂ for at least 10 min before transfer; transfer in less than 30 s per cassette.
3. **Packaging**: Place charged shipper in UN-certified overpack with absorbent material (vermiculite or equivalent).
4. **Documentation**: Shipper's Declaration for Dangerous Goods (UN 1977, LN₂, Class 2.2). Note: Non-hazardous if absorbent material prevents free liquid — confirm with carrier.
5. **Carrier notification**: Advance notice required for dry shipper shipments; FedEx/DHL dangerous goods acceptance required.
6. **Recipient instructions**: Open shipper immediately upon receipt; transfer samples to liquid-phase LN₂ or −80°C freezer within 1 h.

## Insurance Claim Process for Damaged Shipments

### Coverage

All Solarbio international shipments are insured for the full invoice value under marine cargo insurance (Institute Cargo Clauses A). Domestic shipments within China are covered by carrier liability up to RMB 2,000 per package.

### Claim Process

1. **Document damage immediately**: Photograph all damage (outer packaging, inner packaging, product) before signing delivery receipt. Note "DAMAGED — SUBJECT TO INSPECTION" on carrier delivery record.

2. **Notify Solarbio**: Send documentation to <claims@solarbio.com> within 24 h of receipt for domestic shipments; within 7 days for international shipments.

3. **Required documentation**:
   - Order number and invoice copy
   - Photographs of damaged packaging and products (minimum 3 angles)
   - Carrier delivery receipt with damage notation
   - Written description of damage and estimated value (include product SKUs)
   - Temperature logger data (if cold chain shipment)
   - Carrier incident report number (if assigned)

4. **Solarbio action**:
   - Acknowledgment within 4 business hours
   - Replacement order initiated within 1 business day (if product is in stock)
   - Insurance claim filed with carrier within 48 h
   - Customer not of replacement tracking number within 2 business days

5. **Resolution timeline**:
   - Replacement product dispatched: within 5 business days
   - Insurance claim settled: 14–60 days (depending on carrier and region)

## Temperature Data Logger Specification and Interpretation Guide

### Logger Specifications

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Logger type | USB-connected single-use or reusable (Elpro LIBERO, Testo 184 T3, or equivalent) |
| Temperature range | −30°C to +70°C (standard); −80°C to +50°C (cryo variant) |
| Accuracy | ±0.5°C (−20°C to +40°C); ±1.0°C (−80°C to −20°C) |
| Resolution | 0.1°C |
| Logging interval | 1–10 minutes (set according to transport duration) |
| Memory capacity | 16,000–40,000 data points |
| Battery life | 1–2 years (single-use); 3–5 years (reusable, rechargeable) |
| Certification | Factory calibration certificate (NIST-traceable or equivalent) |

### Interpreting Temperature Logger Data

| Pattern | Interpretation | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Stable within range (±1°C of set point) | Valid cold chain | Product approved for use |
| Gradual drift >2°C/h | Insulation failure or gel pack depletion | Performance verification recommended |
| Abrupt spike >5°C for >15 min | Door opened during transport or mishandling | QC evaluation required |
| Below −20°C for 2–8°C products | Freezing excursion | Test for cryo-damage; many ELISA kits survive single freeze-thaw |
| Above 8°C for 2–8°C products for >2 h | Temperature abuse | Replacement likely needed (contact Solarbio) |
| Multiple alternating freeze-thaw events | Severe thermal cycling | Product compromised; replacement required |

## Seasonal Shipping Risk Mitigation

| Season | Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Summer (June–August) — domestic | Ambient temp >35°C | Use cold pack for 2–8°C products; dry ice for −20°C products; avoid Friday shipping |
| Summer — international | Extended customs clearance | Extra dry ice (>8 kg); ship Monday–Tuesday only |
| Summer — equipment failure risk | Carrier depot temperature spikes | Request temperature monitoring with real-time alerts (add $15); upgrade to express service |
| Winter (December–February) — domestic | Freezing damage (−15°C to 0°C) | Insulate cold pack shipments; use phase-change materials not regular frozen gel packs; add freeze-warning indicator |
| Winter — international | Airport tarmac delays (de-icing) | Add freeze-warming indicator; ship with UPS/FedEx cold chain service level |
| Rainy season (April–June, parts of Asia) | Moisture damage | Waterproof inner bag; silica gel desiccant (10 g per liter of box volume) inside outer box |
| Rainy season — humid tropics | Mold growth on packaging | Use vacuum-sealed reagent vials for high-humidity destinations |
| Holiday season (Nov–Dec) | Carrier volume backlog | Order 5–10 days earlier than standard lead time; upgrade to priority shipping |
