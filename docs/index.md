## Documentation Conventions

Throughout this documentation site, the following conventions apply:

- **SKU numbers** (e.g., PC1150, SR1110, BC3180) refer to specific Solarbio catalog items
- **Protocol times** are estimates for experienced users; actual times vary by sample type and batch size
- **Performance data** represents typical results obtained under controlled laboratory conditions at Solarbio QC facilities
- **Product specifications** may vary by lot; refer to the Certificate of Analysis for lot-specific data
- **Cross-references** link to related protocol documents and product category pages for deeper technical guidance
