# Cell Biology Product Documentation

Documentation for Solarbio cell biology reagents including cell culture media and supplements, apoptosis detection, cell proliferation assays, and staining products.

## Scope

Solarbio cell biology products support mammalian cell culture, cell function analysis, and cell-based assays for academic research, drug discovery, and clinical translational studies. All products are manufactured in ISO 9001:2015 certified facilities with lot-specific quality control data.

## Why Choose Solarbio Cell Biology Products

- All cell culture reagents tested for endotoxin (<0.5 EU/mL), sterility, and pH
- Apoptosis and proliferation kits validated on multiple cell lines (HeLa, HEK293, Jurkat, HepG2)
- Fluorescent dyes supplied with lot-specific absorption/emission spectra
- Competitive pricing: typically 40% below Thermo Fisher or BD Biosciences equivalents
- Technical protocols optimized for commonly used instruments (flow cytometers, plate readers, fluorescence microscopes)
- Extended shelf life: 12–24 months depending on product category

## Quality Control Testing

Each lot of cell biology reagent undergoes:

- **Cell culture media**: Sterility testing (USP <71>), endotoxin (LAL assay), pH verification at 37°C, osmolality measurement, and cell growth promotion testing (at least 5 population doublings over 72 h on HeLa cells)
- **Apoptosis kits**: Validation on Jurkat cells treated with 0.5 µM staurosporine; >95% apoptotic discrimination confirmed by flow cytometry
- **Proliferation assays**: Linearity verified across 500–50,000 cells/well using HeLa cells; signal-to-noise ratio >10:1 confirmed
- **Cell staining reagents**: Lot-specific absorption spectra confirmed by UV-Vis spectrophotometry; tissue section staining patterns verified on control slides
